Privacy Policy

Pet Health: Vaccine Records · Last updated 28 September 2026

In short: Pet Health does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device, in encrypted storage, unless you choose to share or export it yourself.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Pet Health iOS and Android application ("the app"), published by Sohaib Zahid. It explains what happens to information you put into the app.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information or pet information. Specifically, the app contains:

  • no user accounts, sign-in or registration
  • no analytics, telemetry or usage tracking
  • no advertising or advertising identifiers
  • no crash reporting service
  • no third-party trackers of any kind

The app makes no network requests of its own. It functions completely offline, including in aeroplane mode.

3. What you store, and where it lives

Information you enter — pet names, species, breed, date of birth, microchip numbers, vaccinations, medications, vet visits, weight entries, grooming records, notes, carer contacts and any photographs you take of documents — is written only to your own device.

This data is held in a database encrypted with SQLCipher (256-bit AES). The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore, marked so that it never leaves that device and is not included in cloud keychain sync.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to photograph a vaccination card or vet paperwork. Photographs are saved to the app's private storage on your device.
  • Photo library — only when you choose to attach an existing photo of your pet or a record.
  • Notifications — only if you enable reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally by your device. No push server is involved and we never see them.

You can decline or later withdraw any of these in your device settings. The app continues to work without them.

5. When information leaves your device

Only when you deliberately send it. The app can produce:

  • a proof-of-vaccination PDF
  • a full health summary PDF
  • a calendar (.ics) file
  • a backup file containing your records

Each is created on your device and handed to your operating system's share sheet. You decide where it goes. Once you send a file, this policy no longer governs it — the app that receives it does.

Please note: the backup file is not encrypted and is not protected by a password. It contains your records in readable form, including microchip numbers. Store it somewhere you consider private, and take care when emailing it or placing it in shared cloud storage.

6. Subscriptions

Pet Health Pro is sold through Apple's App Store and Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by the store — we never see or receive your payment details, card number or billing address. Your subscription status is confirmed by the store on your device.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

8. Deleting your information

Because nothing is held anywhere but your device, there is no account to close and nothing to request from us. You can delete individual pets and records inside the app, use "Reset app data" to remove everything at once, or uninstall the app, which removes its storage entirely.

Deleting the app or resetting its data is permanent. If you have no backup file, the records cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data, that change will be stated here and in the app before it takes effect.
