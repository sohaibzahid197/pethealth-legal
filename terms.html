1. What the app is

Pet Health is a personal record-keeping tool. It stores information you enter about your animals and calculates when routine treatments are next due, based on dates and intervals you supply.

The app is not veterinary advice. It does not diagnose, treat or make clinical recommendations, and it is not a substitute for a qualified veterinary professional. Due dates are arithmetic based on what you entered — they are not medical guidance and may not match your vet's advice, your local regulations, or the requirements of a specific kennel, airline or border authority. Always confirm with your vet or the relevant authority. If your animal is unwell, contact a vet.

The app stores data on your device only. There is no cloud copy and no account. This means:
